# Illustrative stock photography

All assets in this folder are temporary illustrations. They are **not photographs of Bastide Horizons Wellness**. Do not represent pictured properties, rooms, or facilities as this business. Replace them with client-approved photography before making property-specific visual claims.

Images are self-hosted WebP at 640, 1200 and 1920 pixels wide. Their aspect ratio is preserved. `manifest.json` records dimensions, source, photographer, license and variant files.
